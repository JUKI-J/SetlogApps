Fencely

  • Fencely has no accounts. The app itself does not collect personal information. Your puzzle progress, records and settings are stored only on your device.
  • Purchases. Fencely Plus subscriptions, Fencely Lifetime (a one-time purchase) and hint credit packs are purchased and processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel them; you can manage or cancel in your Apple Account settings. We learn only the purchase status that the App Store provides to the app on your device.
  • Advertising. Without Fencely Plus or Fencely Lifetime, the app shows interstitial ads and, only when you choose to watch one to earn hint credits, rewarded ads, all served by Google AdMob. To serve ads, limit how often they appear, prevent fraud and measure performance, Google's SDK may process an app-scoped device identifier, your IP address (used to estimate a general location), information about the ads you see and how you interact with them, and diagnostic data such as performance and crash information. When the app starts, Google's consent SDK also contacts Google to determine whether regional consent rules apply.
  • Fencely does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission, so the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not available to it, and it does not track you across other companies' apps and websites.
  • Ads in Fencely are non-personalized. Every ad request is sent with Google's age-restricted treatment and a maximum ad content rating of G.
  • Fencely is a general-audience app and is not directed primarily at children.
  • While a Fencely Plus subscription is active, and after you buy Fencely Lifetime, the app does not request ads. If a subscription ends, ads can appear again.
  • Hints. Your hint credits, how many free daily hints you have used, and your hint purchase history (the transaction identifier, product and quantity of each hint credit pack you bought) are stored on your device and, if iCloud is turned on, in your own iCloud key-value storage. They are used only to restore your hint credits after you reinstall the app or move to a new device. This data is not sent to our servers - we do not operate a server for the app - and we cannot see it. If you turn iCloud off for the app, it stays on your device only.
  • Crash reports. We do not run our own crash reporting. Apple may collect crash data from users who opt in to share it with developers; it is governed by Apple's policies.
  • Advertising data is processed by Google LLC and may be transferred to and stored on servers outside your country. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • We do not hold this data ourselves. Retention is governed by Google's policies. To exercise your privacy rights, or to report an inappropriate ad, use the info (ⓘ) mark on the ad or contact us through setlog.net/support
  • Fencely에는 계정이 없습니다. 앱 자체는 개인정보를 수집하지 않습니다. 퍼즐 진행·기록·설정은 기기에만 저장됩니다.
  • 구매. Fencely Plus 구독, 평생 이용권(일회성 구매), 힌트 크레딧은 Apple이 판매·처리하며, 저희는 결제 정보를 받지 않습니다. 구독은 해지하기 전까지 자동으로 갱신되며, Apple 계정 설정에서 관리하거나 해지할 수 있습니다. 저희는 App Store가 기기의 앱에 알려 주는 구매 상태만 확인합니다.
